Our Pricing Philosophy

The right solution depends on your goals.

The right digital solution depends on your goals, complexity, audience, technology requirements and long-term needs.

Final pricing can depend on project scope, functionality, integrations, design complexity, custom software requirements, AI features, database needs, authentication, payment systems, cloud infrastructure, third-party services, content, security requirements, maintenance, timeline, support, localization, number of users and organization size.

This page is designed to be transparent about that without pretending every project can be priced from a simple table.

    Payment

    How payment works

    Payment terms are confirmed in the project agreement — not assumed.

    Discovery→ Scope→ Quote→ Agreement→ Initial Payment→ Development→ Testing→ Delivery→ Final Payment→ Support

    Exact percentages and stages are set in the project agreement. Nothing is charged before you have approved the scope.

    Trust

    Clear scope. Clear expectations.

    • Requirements are discussed before final pricing.
    • Scope changes may affect pricing — they are discussed before implementation.
    • External costs are identified up front.
    • Deliverables are defined in writing.
    • Timelines depend on scope and are agreed together.
    • Major changes are agreed before implementation.

    We do not promise "no extra costs ever" — we promise clarity on what is included and what is not.
