Who He Is

A builder shaped by many disciplines.

NIYIBIGIRA Theoneste is a Rwandan technology creator, developer, designer and educator building digital products that connect technology, education, creativity and opportunity.

His work spans web development, educational technology, digital platforms, graphic design, music and community leadership. He is the founder and owner of Ikaze Digital, and the creator behind projects including the Rwanda Tech Learning Hub and his personal portfolio. He also collaborates on educational and community platforms — such as TTC Muhanga Connect — where he contributes as a collaborator, not as owner.

His approach is simple: learn continuously, build useful things, test honestly, improve patiently, and share what works.

Music & Piano

Niyibigira Pianist

Music — particularly piano and solfège — has been one of the longest threads in his creative life. It developed discipline, listening, timing, structure, patience and communication.

At TTC Muhanga, he helped lead the Music Club, whose motto is:

“We learn the language of music and we speak through the piano.”

A Note From the Founder

Why I build.

I grew up between music, art and school. Piano taught me patience. Drawing taught me to look closely. Teaching showed me how knowledge actually moves between people. Writing taught me to make ideas clear.

Technology is where all of that meets. It lets me take everything I learned in those disciplines and turn it into something people can use — a platform, a tool, a place to learn.

I build because Rwanda and Africa don't need more people consuming technology. We need more people building it, teaching it and improving it. That is what Ikaze Digital is for.

— NIYIBIGIRA Theoneste
Founder & Owner, Ikaze Digital
