01Scope

This policy explains how IKAZE DIGITAL handles personal information across:

  • The IKAZE DIGITAL website (ikaze-digital.vercel.app and any custom domain it is served from)
  • Related pages including services, ecosystem, portfolio, pricing, about, contact, start-project, tools, opportunities, insights, product-details
  • Forms you submit through the site (contact, project requests, pricing, early-access, feedback, privacy requests)
  • Future IKAZE DIGITAL digital products where this policy is explicitly adopted
i

Separate products may eventually have additional privacy terms. If a future product needs its own policy, we will publish it alongside that product and link to it clearly.

This policy is written to be readable across borders. It is not legal advice and does not replace formal legal review where required.

02Information We Collect

We aim to collect only what is reasonably needed. The exact information collected depends on what you use.

A. Information you provide

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (where you choose to provide it)
  • Country
  • Organization or company name (where relevant)
  • Role or user type (where you select one)
  • Project information — project type, service, goals, audience, budget range, timeline, references
  • Messages and free-text content you write in forms
  • Feedback and preferences you choose to share

B. Account information

If accounts are introduced for a given product or page:

  • Name and email associated with the account
  • Authentication provider (for example, email/password or Google sign-in — only providers actually enabled)
  • Account identifier assigned by the authentication system
  • Basic profile information you choose to provide

As of the current version of this policy, general account registration is not enabled across the main IKAZE DIGITAL website. Where a product enables accounts, it will state so.

C. Project and request information

When you submit a project or pricing request, we collect the details you enter so we can understand the project and respond. Do not submit information that is not relevant to your request.

D. Technical information

Depending on the technologies enabled, we may receive limited technical information such as:

  • IP address (as seen by our hosting provider)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Approximate technical location (from IP, not precise GPS unless a feature explicitly requests it)
  • Language setting
  • Referring page
  • Timestamps of requests
  • Error information when the site fails to render or a request fails

We do not claim all of the above are always collected. What is actually logged depends on the hosting provider and the specific services in use.

03How Information Is Collected

Information reaches us in two ways:

  • Information you provide directly — through forms, direct email, or contact channels.
  • Information generated by using the site — such as technical logs produced by the hosting infrastructure when your browser requests pages.

Common collection points include:

  • The contact form
  • The start-a-project form
  • Pricing and estimate requests
  • Early-access and interest forms
  • Feedback submissions
  • Direct email to the addresses listed on this site
  • Cookies and local storage (see sections 07 and 09)
  • Server logs at the hosting level
  • Analytics, where enabled (see section 14)

04Why We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes — and only where relevant to your interaction:

Providing services

To respond to your request and, where agreed, to deliver work.

Communication

To reply to inquiries and discuss projects you have asked us about.

Project planning

To understand requirements and prepare estimates or proposals.

Account management

To authenticate users and maintain account functionality where a product enables accounts.

Security

To detect abuse, fraud, unauthorized access and other security issues.

Website improvement

To understand technical performance and improve usability.

Product development

To understand requests and improve future products.

Legal and operational requirements

Where necessary and legally appropriate.

We do not use your information for a purpose the website does not perform.

05Legal Basis & User Choices

Different laws describe lawful processing in different ways. Rather than pretend one framework applies to every visitor, we describe the concepts generally:

  • Consent — where you have clearly agreed to something specific
  • Contractual necessity — where processing is needed to deliver something you requested
  • Legitimate interests — where processing supports reasonable operational or security needs
  • Legal obligation — where a specific law requires a specific action
  • Other applicable legal grounds — as defined by the law that applies to you

Where you have a choice, we try to make that choice clear. Where a field is optional, it is marked as such in the form.

06Consent

Where we ask for consent, we aim to:

  • Explain what you are consenting to in plain language
  • Keep optional consent (such as marketing) separate from necessary processing
  • Allow consent to be withdrawn where applicable

We do not:

  • Pre-check optional marketing boxes
  • Use confusing wording to imply consent was given
  • Hide the option to decline

07Cookies & Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) may be used for:

Essential technologies

  • Security
  • Session handling where an account feature exists
  • Preferences you set (such as language or theme)
  • Core site functionality

Preference technologies

  • Remembering your language selection
  • Remembering interface choices you have made

Analytics technologies

Enabled only if analytics are actually configured (see section 14). If no analytics are active, this category does not apply.

Marketing technologies

We do not currently use advertising or marketing cookies on this site.

08Cookie Control

At this time, the IKAZE DIGITAL site uses essential and preference cookies only. There is no advertising or third-party tracking that requires a cookie consent banner.

⚙

Manage cookies in your browser. You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser's privacy settings. Doing so may reset preferences such as language selection.

If a consent management interface is added later, it will be provided here. It is not shown as a fake control now.

09Local Storage

Browser local storage may be used for non-sensitive things such as:

  • Interface preferences
  • Language selection
  • Temporary drafts you enter in a form (where supported)
  • Other non-sensitive settings

We do not store passwords, tokens or sensitive information in local storage.

10Firebase & Cloud Services

IKAZE DIGITAL uses Firebase (Google Cloud) for various platform functions as they are introduced, which may include:

  • Authentication
  • Cloud database services (for example, Firestore)
  • File storage (where configured)
  • Hosting of some services
  • Infrastructure related to application functionality and security

The exact set of Firebase services used depends on the specific page or product. When you submit information through a form connected to Firebase, that information is stored in cloud infrastructure subject to the security rules configured for that service.

No Firebase Admin credentials or private server keys are ever included in the browser-delivered code. Frontend Firebase configuration (project ID, API key for client SDK) is public by design and protected by security rules.

11Firebase Authentication

Where authentication is enabled, the following methods may be available, depending on the product:

  • Email and password
  • Google sign-in
  • Other providers, if a specific product enables them

Authentication providers process account information according to their own privacy policies. IKAZE DIGITAL does not control these third-party providers — it uses them as a service.

As of this policy version, general IKAZE DIGITAL website access does not require an account. Authentication is reserved for future product functionality.

12Forms & Information You Submit

Information submitted through a form is used for the purpose associated with that form. For example:

  • Contact form: to reply to your message
  • Start-a-project form: to understand your project and prepare a response or proposal
  • Pricing / estimate form: to prepare a quote or discuss pricing
  • Early-access / interest form: to follow up about the relevant product
  • Feedback form: to understand your feedback about a product or page
  • Privacy request form: to process a privacy-related request
!

Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive information.

Avoid sending passwords, financial credentials, government identification numbers, health information, authentication codes, private keys or confidential business secrets through ordinary contact forms unless specifically requested and securely supported.

Submitting a project request does not automatically subscribe you to marketing communications.

13Attachments

Where file attachments are supported, they are collected only for the purpose of understanding your project or request. Please:

  • Send only files relevant to your request
  • Do not upload documents containing unnecessary sensitive personal information
  • Prefer a summary or redacted version if the full document contains private data

Attachment handling depends on the specific storage service configured. Access is limited to those who need it for the request.

14Analytics & Performance

Analytics capabilities may be introduced in the future to understand technical performance and improve the site.

At this time, no third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel) are enabled on the IKAZE DIGITAL site.

If analytics are added later, this section will be updated to describe:

  • Which tool is used
  • What information is collected
  • Why it is used
  • How you can control it

15Third-Party Services

Where third-party services are used to operate parts of the site, they may process information in order to provide that service. Categories include:

  • Hosting providers — for serving the site
  • Firebase / Google Cloud — for authentication, storage and cloud functions where enabled
  • Authentication providers — where sign-in options are enabled
  • Payment processors — only where payment functionality is actually introduced
  • Email or SMS providers — where used for communication
  • AI providers — only where AI features are enabled
  • CDN providers — for font or asset delivery

Third-party providers have their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

Additional third-party services may be introduced as new functionality becomes available, and this section will be updated when they are.

16Payment Information

Payment processing may be introduced for applicable services and products. Details will be provided when payment functionality becomes available.

Where a third-party payment provider processes payments:

  • Payment card data is handled by the payment processor, not stored on IKAZE DIGITAL systems
  • We do not ask users to send card numbers, PINs or payment passwords through ordinary forms or email
  • Only the minimum information needed to reconcile a payment is retained on our side

At this time, no payment provider is active on the IKAZE DIGITAL website.

17AI & Automated Services

Some IKAZE DIGITAL products are planned to include AI-powered functionality. Where AI features exist:

  • Inputs you provide may be processed to generate the requested output
  • You should avoid submitting unnecessary confidential information
  • Specific AI products may have additional terms or privacy information
  • Third-party AI providers may process information where they are used to deliver the feature

We do not claim that user data is or is not used for AI model training, because that depends on the specific provider configuration of a given feature. Where relevant, we will describe the configuration of that feature.

18When Information May Be Shared

We do not share personal information for advertising purposes and we do not sell it. Information may be shared only in the following situations:

  • Service providers — providers necessary to operate a specific service you are using
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting, database, authentication, security and related infrastructure
  • Legal requirements — where required by applicable law or a valid legal request
  • Security — to investigate abuse, fraud or security incidents where appropriate
  • Business changes — if the business structure changes, subject to applicable law

19Data Sale

✓

IKAZE DIGITAL does not sell personal information.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to third parties. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

20Data Retention

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Retention depends on:

  • The purpose of collection
  • The type of information
  • Account status (where accounts exist)
  • Legal requirements
  • Operational needs
  • Security requirements

We do not publish specific retention periods unless they are formally established, because they depend on the specific interaction and any applicable legal obligations.

21Requesting Deletion

Where applicable, you can request deletion of personal information using the request form on this page or by contacting us at the address in section 39.

Some information may not be immediately deletable if:

  • A legal retention obligation applies
  • Security records need to be retained for a period
  • Contractual obligations exist
  • Backups cannot be modified immediately

Where deletion cannot be fully completed immediately, we will explain what remains and why.

22Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights such as:

  • Access — request a copy of personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request deletion, subject to legal retention
  • Restriction — request that processing be limited in certain circumstances
  • Objection — object to certain processing
  • Portability — request a machine-readable copy where applicable
  • Withdraw consent — where processing relies on your consent
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with a relevant authority

These rights are not automatic for every visitor in every jurisdiction. The rights that apply to you depend on your location and the applicable law.

To make a request, use the form in section 38 or contact us directly.

23Verifying Requests

To protect your information, we may need to verify your identity before acting on certain privacy requests. Verification typically means confirming you can access the email address associated with your request.

We will never ask you for:

  • Your password
  • Authentication codes or one-time passwords
  • Full payment card details

We may ask for additional information where necessary to confirm we are acting on the right person's data, but we aim to keep verification as light as possible while still protecting you.

24International Privacy

IKAZE DIGITAL is rooted in Rwanda and may serve users beyond Rwanda through digital services. Data may be processed using infrastructure located in different countries depending on the technologies and providers used.

For example, Firebase and Google Cloud infrastructure may process data in data centers outside Rwanda. Where this happens, the processing is subject to the terms of those providers.

We do not claim specific international transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) unless they have been formally implemented for a specific service.

25Privacy in Our Context

IKAZE DIGITAL aims to build technology responsibly from Rwanda and Africa. That means treating privacy as part of the design of every product, not an afterthought.

Where specific legal frameworks apply — such as Rwanda's data protection framework or international standards — we aim to work in a way that is consistent with them, and we will update this page as specific compliance measures are adopted and verified.

We do not claim formal certification under any specific privacy law unless such certification exists.

26Children's Privacy

The IKAZE DIGITAL website is designed for general audiences — creators, businesses, students and organizations.

  • Users should not submit personal information belonging to children through ordinary contact forms
  • Where an educational product is specifically designed for use by or with children, its privacy approach will be described separately, alongside that product
  • Additional protections may apply to educational services and will be documented in the relevant product's terms

If you believe a child's information has been submitted to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

27How We Protect Information

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the systems involved. These can include:

  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Firebase Security Rules, where Firebase is used
  • Access control limited to those who need it
  • Input validation
  • Secure communication over HTTPS
  • Least-privilege principles
  • Secure backend operations
  • Monitoring where available
  • Abuse prevention
  • Careful credential management
!

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We aim to use safeguards appropriate to the information and systems involved, but we do not claim our systems are "100% secure."

28Security Incidents

If a significant security incident occurs, IKAZE DIGITAL will handle it according to applicable requirements and the circumstances of the incident.

We do not promise a specific notification window here, because that depends on the applicable legal framework and the specific nature of the incident. Where a legal framework requires notification within a specific period, we will follow it.

29Password Security

  • Never share your password
  • Avoid sending passwords through forms, email or chat
  • Enable additional security features where available

IKAZE DIGITAL will never ask you to send your password through email or a contact form.

32Marketing & Communications

We distinguish between:

  • Transactional and project communications — messages necessary to deliver a service or discuss a project you have asked about
  • Optional marketing communications — messages about new products, updates or opportunities

We do not add you to marketing lists automatically. Where optional marketing exists, we ask for consent, keep it separate from other consent, and provide a way to unsubscribe.

33Public & User-Generated Content

Future IKAZE DIGITAL products may include features such as creator profiles, community posts, marketplace listings or reviews.

Where a feature is designed to publish content publicly, information you choose to publish may be visible to other users and, potentially, the wider internet.

Please do not publish through public features:

  • Passwords or authentication codes
  • Private contact information
  • Financial credentials
  • Confidential documents
  • Sensitive personal information about yourself or others

These features are not yet live on the current site. This section is provided so the policy remains accurate when they are introduced.

34Keeping Your Information Accurate
