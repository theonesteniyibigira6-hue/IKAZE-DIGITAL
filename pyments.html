Payment Features

Every capability, honestly labelled.

Infrastructure is being built in phases. Each feature below states its actual status.

◆
In Development

One-time Payments

Services, courses, digital products, cloud services.

Secure checkout initialized on the backend, verified via provider webhooks. Access is granted only after trusted confirmation — never from a browser redirect.

◈
In Development

Subscriptions

Academy, Cloud, Creator memberships.

Recurring billing with real provider-managed subscription state. Grace periods, cancellations and plan changes handled by the provider and validated by the backend.

▣
In Development

Invoices

Real invoice records with unique numbers.

Draft → issued → paid → overdue → void. Every invoice number is generated by the backend. Clients can pay online or view PDF — issued amounts are immutable.

↩
Coming Soon

Refunds

Backend-controlled, provider-verified.

Refund eligibility validated against the original transaction. Full or partial. Never marked successful from the frontend — only from provider confirmation.

◇
Coming Soon

Creator Payouts

Earnings with immutable ledger.

Creator marketplace earnings accrued from verified transactions. Payouts requested by the creator, approved by the backend, settled through the provider.

◐
Coming Soon

Multi-Currency

RWF, USD, EUR, KES, and more.

Currency only enabled when supported by the connected provider. Original currency, amount, settlement currency and exchange rate are recorded — never silently converted.

✦
Coming Soon

Receipts

Generated only after trusted payment confirmation.

Every receipt reflects the backend transaction — customer, transaction ID, invoice, amount, currency, payment method, payment date and status.

▲
Concept

Reconciliation

Compare IKAZE ledger against provider records.

Automated detection of missing, duplicate, or mismatched transactions. Admin review queue. Historical financial records are never silently overwritten.

⌘
Concept

Admin Finance Center

Role-gated access to financial operations.

Finance role separated from technical admin. Least-privilege access with full audit logs. Every privileged financial action is recorded immutably.
